# Apple Dictation alternative for iPhone

Apple Dictation is free, built in, and runs on the iPhone for many languages. For a quick text it is hard to beat. Where people outgrow it: it writes down exactly what you said, filler words and all, and it stops after 30 seconds of silence. Diction is for when you want text you can send as it is.

Diction is a full keyboard for iPhone, written from the ground up, with voice built in. Tap the mic above the keys in any app, speak, and clean text lands at your cursor. You choose where your voice is turned into text: on your iPhone, on your server, or on Diction Cloud.

## Where Apple Dictation is the better pick

It is already on your phone, it needs nothing installed, and it works on the system keyboard you know. For short messages where a raw transcript is fine, keep using it.

## Where Diction is the better pick

- **Text you can send as it is.** Writing Tools remove the ums, fix grammar and punctuation, and format lists.
- **Edit by talking.** Tell it what to change in text already in the field.
- **Long dictation.** Pause to think without being cut off.
- **Choose where it runs.** On the phone, on a server you run, or on Diction Cloud with Live transcription.
