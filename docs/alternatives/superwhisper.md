# Superwhisper alternative for iPhone

Superwhisper is a well-liked dictation app that started on the Mac, with on-device models, custom modes and a lifetime plan. On iPhone it needs iOS 26, and there is no documented way to point it at a speech server you run. If you self-host, or you want a full typing keyboard with voice in it, Diction is built for that.

Diction is a full keyboard for iPhone, written from the ground up, with voice built in. Tap the mic above the keys in any app, speak, and clean text lands at your cursor. You choose where your voice is turned into text: on your iPhone, on your server, or on Diction Cloud.

## Where Superwhisper is the better pick

If you mainly dictate on a Mac and want the same app and modes on your phone, Superwhisper's Mac app is mature, and the lifetime plan suits people who dislike subscriptions.

## Where Diction is the better pick

- **You self-host.** Diction sends your voice to a speech server you run, Whisper included, from any app on the phone. Free, no account, no limits.
- **One keyboard for typing and voice.** Autocorrect and suggestions on the phone, the mic one tap away.
- **Works on iOS 17 and later.**
- **Diction Cloud** has Live transcription and every Writing Tool for $5.99 a month.
