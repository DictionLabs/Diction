# Willow alternative for iPhone

Willow is a fast AI keyboard with strong formatting and a large user base. On iPhone it runs in the cloud, Pro is $15 a month, and its App Store privacy label lists usage data used to track you. If you want dictation that can stay on your phone or hardware you control, Diction is built for that.

Diction is a full keyboard for iPhone, written from the ground up, with voice built in. Tap the mic above the keys in any app, speak, and clean text lands at your cursor. You choose where your voice is turned into text: on your iPhone, on your server, or on Diction Cloud.

## Where Willow is the better pick

If your team wants a managed business plan with enforced zero data retention and admin controls, Willow sells that today.

## Where Diction is the better pick

- **Free with no word limit.** On-device and self-hosted, no account.
- **Your voice, your hardware.** Offline on the phone, or on a server you run. Audio is never stored in any mode, and the app collects no data.
- **You self-host.** Any OpenAI-compatible speech server and any LLM you run, Ollama included.
- **Price.** Diction Cloud is $5.99 a month, less than half of Willow Pro.
