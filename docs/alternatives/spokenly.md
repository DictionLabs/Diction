# Spokenly alternative for iPhone

Spokenly is a privacy-minded dictation app with free on-device models and bring-your-own API keys, and it is good at both. The difference with Diction is the keyboard and the server: Diction is a full typing keyboard with voice built in, and it sends your voice to a speech server you run.

Diction is a full keyboard for iPhone, written from the ground up, with voice built in. Tap the mic above the keys in any app, speak, and clean text lands at your cursor. You choose where your voice is turned into text: on your iPhone, on your server, or on Diction Cloud.

## Side by side

Facts for the iPhone versions, from the App Store and each company's site, checked October 2026.

| | Diction | Spokenly (iPhone) |
|---|---|---|
| Free tier | On-device and self-hosted, no word limit | On-device models and your API keys, unlimited |
| Paid | Diction Cloud $5.99/month or $59.99/year | From $9.99/month |
| Where your voice goes | Your choice: your iPhone, your server, or Diction Cloud | On-device, Spokenly's cloud, or your API provider |
| Self-hosted speech server | Yes, open-source server, `docker compose up`, pair by QR | Not documented for iPhone |
| Bring your own API key | Your server can use any OpenAI-compatible speech or LLM endpoint | Yes: OpenAI, Deepgram, Groq |
| Typing keyboard | Full QWERTY with autocorrect and next-word suggestions | Not documented |
| AI cleanup | Yes: filler words, grammar, formatting | Yes, on Pro |
| Edit text by voice | Yes: "make it shorter", "translate to Spanish" | Not documented for iPhone |
| Dictation languages | 99 | 100+ |
| Account | None needed | None needed |
| App Store privacy label | Data Not Collected | Data Not Linked to You: Usage Data |

## Where Spokenly is the better pick

If you already pay for OpenAI, Deepgram or Groq and want to use those keys directly, Spokenly makes that easy. It also supports iOS 16.4.

## Where Diction is the better pick

- **You self-host.** Run the open-source Diction server at home and dictate into any app from your iPhone, with Whisper or any OpenAI-compatible model, and Writing Tools on an LLM you run.
- **One keyboard for typing and voice.** Autocorrect and suggestions on the phone, the mic one tap away.
- **Diction Cloud is $5.99 a month** with Live transcription and every Writing Tool.
